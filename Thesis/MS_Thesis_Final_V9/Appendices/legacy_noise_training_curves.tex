\section{\acs{ekf} Partial-Observation Training Curves Under Different Measurement Noise Settings}
\label{sec:appendix-noise-training-curves}

These runs are included to illustrate how the \acs{ekf}-based partial-observation training curves changed as the assumed measurement-noise level increased. All cases used $u_{\max} = 2.0~\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $v_{\max} = 1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$, and $v_{\max}^{\mathrm{IC}} = 0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$, albeit with a different control architecture than the one presented in this thesis, since these results do not have the \acs{cbfqp} controller implemented.
