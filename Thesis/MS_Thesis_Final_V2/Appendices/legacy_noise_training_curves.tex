\section{Legacy EKF-BC Training Curves Under Different Measurement Noise Settings}
\label{sec:appendix-legacy-noise-training-curves}

These legacy runs are included to illustrate how the EKF-based behavior-cloning training curves changed as the assumed measurement-noise level increased. All three legacy cases used $u_{\max} = 2.0~\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $v_{\max} = 1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$, and $v_{\max}^{\mathrm{IC}} = 0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$.
