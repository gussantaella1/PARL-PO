\(\mathbf{o}_{\mathrm{full}}\in \mathbb{R}^{5D}\) & Full-state observation \(\big[(\mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{c}),(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{c}),(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{p}_1),\mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2\big]\).\\[2pt]

\(d_2=\tfrac{\|\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{c}\|^2}{R^2}\) & Normalized attacker-to-asset distance.\\
\(r_{\mathrm{rel}}^2=\tfrac{\|\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{p}_1\|^2}{R^2}\) & Normalized squared defender-attacker separation.\\
\(r_{\mathrm{cap}}>0\) & Capture/intercept radius.\\
\(d_{\mathrm{dock}}=\tfrac{\max\{0,\|\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{p}_1\|-r_{\mathrm{cap}}\}}{R}\) & Normalized docking/separation term used in reward shaping.\\
\(\beta_{\mathrm{cap}},\beta_{\mathrm{wall}}\) & Terminal reward magnitudes for capture and boundary events.\\
\(k_{\mathrm{pos}},k_{\mathrm{dock}}\) & Step-reward weights.\\[2pt]

\(v_{\max}\) & Velocity bound enforced by the safety filter.\\
\(\alpha_{\mathrm{CBF}}\) & Control barrier function gain.\\
\(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{nom}},\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{cmd}},\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{app}}\) & Nominal, squashed, and applied control inputs.\\
\(P_{\mathrm{pos}}=[I~0]\in\mathbb{R}^{D\times 2D}\) & Position projector; \(F=P_{\mathrm{pos}}B_d\).\\
\(K=(F^\top F+\lambda_{\mathrm{ridge}} I)^{-1}F^\top\) & Ridge gain used in the one-step center-pull prior.\\
\(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{center}}=-K(P_{\mathrm{pos}}A_d\mathbf{x}_2-\mathbf{c})\) & One-step center-pull term in the rule-based attacker.\\
\(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{repulse}}=\tfrac{g\,(\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{p}_1)}{(\max\{r_{\min}^2,\|\mathbf{p}_2-\mathbf{p}_1\|^2\}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{rep}})^{3/2}}\) & Smooth repulsion term in the rule-based attacker.\\
\(w_c,w_r,w_d\) & Weights in the rule-based attacker.\\[2pt]

\(\gamma \in (0,1]\) & Discount factor.\\
\(\lambda_{\mathrm{GAE}}\in[0,1]\) & Generalized advantage estimation trace parameter.\\
\(\epsilon_{\mathrm{PPO}}\) & PPO clipping half-width.\\
\(c_V, c_{\mathcal{H}}\) & Value-loss and entropy regularization coefficients.\\
\(\boldsymbol{\mu}_\theta(\cdot),\boldsymbol{\sigma}_\theta(\cdot)\) & Mean and diagonal standard deviation of the Gaussian policy.\\
\(N_{\mathrm{env}},H,B\) & Number of environments, rollout horizon, and batch size, with \(B=N_{\mathrm{env}}H\).\\
\(U,E\) & PPO updates per policy and epochs per update.\\
\(\theta,\psi\) & Policy and value-function parameters.\\

\section{Implementation terminology}
\label{appendix:methodology-implementation-terminology}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Actor:} learned residual multi-layer-perceptron (MLP) on \(\phi\) with prior blend \(\beta_{\mathrm{def}}\in[0,1]\), diagonal standard deviation \(\boldsymbol{\sigma}=\exp(\log\boldsymbol{\sigma})\).  The log-standard-deviation terms are learned together with the policy.
\item \textbf{Squash:} Mapping of a raw  acceleration to a defined actuation limit, \(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{cmd}} = u_{\max}\tanh(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{raw}})\), with exact log-Jacobian correction. This lets the policy explore continuously while respecting the acceleration bound used by the actuators.
\item \textbf{VecEnv:} Single-process vectorization. Instead of running one rollout at a time, we run \(N_{\mathrm{env}}\) rollouts in parallel. When one rollout terminates, it is reset immediately so that the batch keeps the same tensor shape during training.

\item \textbf{Fixed-opponent phase:} Training phase where one agent is learning while the opponent policy is kept fixed. This is used so that the learning problem is less unstable than if both agents were changing at the same time.

\item \textbf{Estimator-enabled rollout:} a rollout where the policy does not observe the opponent's true state directly, but instead receives the opponent state estimated by the EKF. Rewards and terminal conditions are still computed from the true simulated state.

\item \textbf{Teacher and student policies:} the teacher is the policy trained with full-state information. The student is the estimator-backed policy that is trained to imitate the teacher when only bearings-only EKF partial observations are available.
\end{itemize}
