The results trained under an HCW linear-time-invariant dynamics model hold
within the context of an elliptical linear-time-variant dynamics model with almost
equal performance.
We think that this is due to a couple of things: 1) the fact that the policies are
trained on observations that don’t depend on the dynamics model itself, and rather
just the states of each agent and their adversary, and 2) the HCW or Elliptical LTV
dynamics objectively don’t provide that much environmental acceleration onto the
agents relative to how much actuation limits we’re giving each agent.
Ergo, the agents may be able to offset whatever environmental dynamics given
the actuation limits that we’re giving them in this work. If this work were to be
translated to real life ultra low thrust ion thrusters, like those that can be found in
CubeSats and small satellites of the sort in LEO, this is one of the things I’d be most
careful with.
