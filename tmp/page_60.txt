6.2 Metrics and Evaluation
We evaluate each training policy matchup
(πdef,0 vsπ att,1,π def,1 vsπ att,1,π def,1 vsπ att,2, andπ def,2 vsπ att,2)
by testing them over a fixed time horizonT through the monte calro testing.
Each simulation starts from randomized and dispersed in initial conditions for both
agents within fixed boundaries for the arena. In each simulation rollout, we report
the following metrics:
Simulation outcome:
The main metric we’re interested in the simulation is the outcome of the game:
whether the defender won, whether the attacker won, or if they tie within the limits
fo the time horizon. As described in section??, we count a defender win when the
defender captures the attacker or when the attacker leaves the arena. Moreover, we
count an attacker win when the attacker reaches the object of interest (OI), when the
defender crashes accidentally into the OI, and when the defender leaves the arena.
