6.5 Ideas for future development
For follow ups on the work presented on this thesis, however, I think that there
are a number of areas that could be researched to expand on the scopes of what is
presented here. Some ideas for this include.
• 1) Modifying the reward functions to include rewards for causing poor measure-
ments on opponents (i.e: By rewarding for the higher the ??? in the ??? matrix
of the opponent would be per certain dynamics and the sort).
• 2) Allowing the asset to be able to move along the dynamics model instead of
having it be static at the chief of the orbit.
• 3) Adding more elaborate, realistic control models to simulate the actuators on
board the spacecraft with high fidelity.
It must be noted that the work presented in this thesis is meant to be an
extensive proof of concept of certain ideas that could make RL-based GNC interesting
in theory and simulation. The zero-sum game presented in this work is just meant to
allow us to be a showcase how these RL can be used, whereas there are many other
potential uses for these technologies.
