To tackle this issue, we opt for the Picard–Chebyshev
iteration method [41] instead of the Runge–Kutta method
to solve the relative dynamics described by (5) for orbit
propagation. More specifically, the time horizon of orbit
propagation is transformed to the interval [−1, 1], and a
best square approximation is conducted on the basis of the
first Chebyshev polynomial. Furthermore, the Chebyshev
series approximation yields a discrete representation of the
differential equations, which can be further transformed into
an initial value problem via Picard iteration theory. The
mathematical iteration formulas proposed in [41] have been
validated for convergence, ensuring the effectiveness of the
method.
